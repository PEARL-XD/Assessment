# Digital Marketing Doctor — Website review and landing-page concept

The existing Digital Marketing Doctor website presents the company professionally, with a clear brand identity, detailed service information and an extensive portfolio. I used these strengths as the starting point for my assessment.

**The main opportunity I identified** was to make the next step clearer for a first-time visitor. The homepage offers several navigation options and detailed sections, which give visitors plenty of information to scan before making an enquiry. During my review, the Google Ads audit button also led to contact information in the footer. I explored a more direct route to an audit request in my concept.

**What I changed and why:**

- **Simplified navigation and shorter copy:** to help visitors understand the offering and find a relevant section more quickly.
- **Three featured client examples and five service tabs:** to present a focused selection of the company’s work while keeping its service offering easy to explore.
- **A direct audit form:** to make the next action explicit. The website address and selected service carry into the form to reduce repeated input. Submission is demonstrated on the frontend only.
- **Readable typography, responsive layouts and subtle animation:** to demonstrate attention to reading comfort, interaction and visual detail. Keyboard support and reduced-motion handling are also included.

**Why my design looks different:** I chose a navy and lavender palette, larger typography and layered portfolio previews to explore an alternative visual direction. This gave me an opportunity to demonstrate my React development, layout, responsive design and interaction skills while keeping the company’s services and published work central to the page. The result is a creative interpretation for this assessment, rather than a claim that the existing professional website needs to be replaced.

**Review basis:** visual and usability observations from 10 October 2026, covering the homepage, Google Ads page and contact page, including desktop and mobile views. This is a frontend concept; no conversion improvement has been measured. Client imagery and reported figures come from the original website; see `ASSETS.md` for references. Proposed audit and process copy are part of the concept.
